# Brag Plan: Petitati

## What is this app?
A live pet-supply shop (petitati.vercel.app) that speaks English, Turkish and Persian (RTL), with a full back office: orders, delivery runs, finance and a Design editor. Underneath, it's a multi-store platform, so one codebase can run any number of branded shops.

## The angle
"Everything your pet loves," and everything the shopkeeper needs. The video goes from the storefront to three languages to the admin that runs it, then lands on the platform claim: one codebase, every store. It shows the real live site (screenshots captured from production), not mockups.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The paw logo pops onto the store's eggshell background, then the store's own headline slams in word by word: **"Everything your pet loves."** It holds long enough to read.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The live home page in a browser frame, scrolling from the cat-and-dog hero down through categories and products.
- Three languages arrive one by one: English desktop, Türkçe desktop, فارسی phone (right-to-left).
- The admin: a cursor clicks the "Delivered" tab on the Orders screen, then the Design editor slides in with its live preview.

## Outro / punchline
"One codebase. Every store." Then the Petitati lockup and the URL.

## User flow worth showing
Shopper lands on the home page → browses (scroll) → switches language. Staff opens Orders → filters by status → edits the storefront in Design with live preview.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: warm, clean shop launch, like a friendly product film for a neighbourhood pet shop
- Interpretation: smooth slides and holds, feature-card clarity, brand teal and paw orange on eggshell, no jokes forced

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #faf8f3 (store eggshell)
- Accent: #157fa1 (primary teal), #f7a83b (paw orange)
- Text: #1b2a33
- Display font: Manrope 800 (store font); Persian: Vazirmatn
- Body font: Manrope 500
- Strongest visual element: the cat-and-dog hero with "Everything your pet loves"

## Share copy (draft)
Petitati is live: a pet shop in English, Türkçe and فارسی, with its own orders, delivery and design admin, and one codebase that can run every store.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with light, consistent UI accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120 BPM, most energetic)
- Music treatment: starts at 0, volume ~0.35, fade out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cue 16.02s → outro lockup. Beat grid (0.5s): language cards on every other beat at 7.02 / 8.02 / 9.02 so labels can be read.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (edit is already busy with real UI; skipped deliberately)
- SFX posture: moderate, 5-6 cues, low HF-risk files
- Audio-coupled moments: logo pop, card slides, cursor click, outro bell
- Restraint rule: no stacked hits, nothing louder than 0.8

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 2.5s
Paw mark pops at centre-top, then "Everything your pet loves." word by word (store headline, verbatim). Holds ~1s settled.
Sequential/interaction: words arrive one by one
Audio intent: friendly opener
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop on the paw pop
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The live shop — 4s
Kicker "Live at petitati.vercel.app". Browser card with the real home page rises in, then scrolls down the page (hero → categories → products).
Sequential/interaction: simulated scroll
Audio intent: lift
Audio-coupled idea: card slide as the browser rises
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Three languages — 4s
Title "Speaks three languages." EN desktop, TR desktop, FA phone cards arrive on beats 7.02 / 8.02 / 9.02 with labels English / Türkçe / فارسی. Full set holds to 10.5.
Sequential/interaction: yes, three cards one by one
Audio-coupled idea: card place per card
Transition mood: slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The back office — 5s
Kicker "And runs from one admin." Orders screen in the browser card; cursor glides to "Delivered" and clicks (~12.0). Then the Design editor slides in over it (~13.2) with the caption "Orders · Delivery · Design".
Sequential/interaction: simulated click
Audio-coupled idea: mouse click, card slide
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 4.5s
"One codebase. Every store." lands on the 16.02 strong cue, then logo lockup + petitati.vercel.app. Music fades out.
Audio-coupled idea: bell on the lockup
Transition mood: hold to end
